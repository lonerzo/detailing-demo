Frequently asked questions

How do I book a lesson?

Pick a lesson or a package above, add it to your request, choose a date and time, and send the request. The school will call you back to confirm.

Can I add a car for the road test?

Yes. Add “Car for road test day” to your booking when you pick the road test prep, and the school will confirm the details.

How and when do I pay?

There is no online payment on this site. The school confirms the final price and how to pay when they contact you.

Can I change or cancel my lesson?

Yes. Call the school at 850-530-9237 and they will move or cancel your booking.

Privacy policy

Updated November 22, 2026. Driving school and tutoring service Grandest Line (Pensacola, FL).

1. General provisions

This policy describes what data we receive when you leave a request on the site, why we need it, and how you can manage it. By leaving a request, you agree to the terms of this policy.

2. What data we collect

  • name, phone number, and email - from the request form;
  • preferred date and time, and your comment on the booking;
  • booking contents: the lessons picked, extras, and the estimate.

We do not ask for passport details, bank card details, or any other information that is not needed to complete the booking. There is no online payment on the site.

3. Why we need this data

  • to contact you and confirm the final price and lesson time;
  • to prepare for the lesson and take your wishes from the comment into account;
  • to meet our obligations for the booking.

We do not use your data for marketing mailings without your separate consent.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing. Only the school can see a request - only as much as is needed to complete your booking. Data may be given to government authorities only in cases directly required by law.

5. Storage and protection

Data is stored no longer than needed to complete the booking, then deleted. We take reasonable organizational and technical measures to keep the data from reaching outsiders.

6. Your rights

You can find out at any time what data of yours we hold, ask to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent to processing. To do so, just call the school phone number: 850-530-9237. We carry out a deletion request after the booking ends, unless a longer storage period is required by law.

7. Policy changes

If we change this policy, the current version will be published on this page with a new date. By continuing to use the site after a change, you agree to the updated terms.
