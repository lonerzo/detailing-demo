How cleaning works

From request to dry furniture - six steps. All the work happens at your home, there is no need to take furniture anywhere.

1

Request and photos

You build your request in the calculator above and, if you can, attach photos of the furniture. From the photos the cleaner can see the scope of work and the state of the upholstery in advance.

2

Price and time confirmation

The cleaner contacts you, clarifies the details, and confirms the final price and visit time. Everything is fixed before arrival - no price surprises on the day.

3

Visit and inspection

The cleaner arrives with equipment at the agreed time and inspects the fabric on site. All we need from you is a power outlet and access to water.

4

Choosing the solution

The solution is matched to your exact upholstery material and first tested on a hidden spot. We use hypoallergenic products that are safe for children and pets once dry.

5

Deep cleaning

The solution is applied to the upholstery and pulled out together with the dirt by a professional extractor, deep from the filling. Cleaning usually takes 1.5 to 3 hours.

6

Accepting the work and payment

You inspect the result and pay only after accepting the work. Furniture dries fully in 12-24 hours, and the 14-day guarantee keeps working after the visit.

Frequently asked questions

How long does cleaning take?

Usually 1.5 to 3 hours. The exact time depends on the size of the furniture, the number of items, and how dirty they are: a sofa with heavy stains takes longer than a single armchair.

When can I use the furniture after cleaning?

Full drying takes 12-24 hours. Airing out the room speeds it up noticeably. It is best to sleep on a mattress or sit on a sofa only after it is fully dry.

Are the cleaning products safe for children and pets?

Yes, we use hypoallergenic products, and after cleaning the residue is extracted together with the dirty water. Children and pets should return to the furniture only after it is fully dry.

What should I prepare before the cleaner arrives?

Almost nothing: we need clear access to the furniture, a power outlet, and water access for the equipment. It is best to clear small items off the furniture in advance, but there is no need to move heavy furniture.

How and when do I pay?

There is no prepayment. The final price is confirmed by phone before the visit, and you pay the cleaner after you have accepted the finished work.

What if a stain does not come out completely?

Most fresh stains come out completely, but to be honest: old stains and marks from DIY cleaning attempts sometimes stay partly visible, and the cleaner will tell you before starting. And if a flaw caused by us shows up within 14 days after cleaning, a return visit and fix are free under the guarantee.

Do you travel outside the city?

Yes, we cover the city and nearby suburbs, and we travel outside the city for a flat extra fee - it shows in the calculator right away when you check “Travel outside the city” in your request.

Privacy policy

Updated November 12, 2026. On-site furniture cleaning service Softly (St. Petersburg, FL).

1. General provisions

This policy describes what data we receive when you leave a request on the site, why we need it, and how you can manage it. By leaving a request, you agree to the terms of this policy.

2. What data we collect

  • name and phone number - from the request form;
  • visit address, preferred date and time, and your comment on the order;
  • order contents: the furniture picked, cleaning options, and the estimate;
  • photos of the furniture that you attach to the request if you wish.

We do not ask for passport details, bank card details, or any other information that is not needed to complete the order. There is no online payment on the site.

3. Why we need this data

  • to contact you and confirm the final price and visit time;
  • to come to the right address and clean the furniture you picked;
  • to take your wishes from the comment into account and price the work from the photos;
  • to meet our guarantee obligations within 14 days after cleaning.

We do not use your data for marketing mailings without your separate consent.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing. Only the cleaner and the service owner can see a request - only as much as is needed to complete your order. Data may be given to government authorities only in cases directly required by law.

5. Storage and protection

Data is stored no longer than needed to complete the order and the guarantee obligations, then deleted. We take reasonable organizational and technical measures to keep the data from reaching outsiders.

6. Your rights

You can find out at any time what data of yours we hold, ask to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent to processing. To do so, just call the service phone number: (555) 123-4567. We carry out a deletion request after the order and the guarantee period end, unless a longer storage period is required by law.

7. Policy changes

If we change this policy, the current version will be published on this page with a new date. By continuing to use the site after a change, you agree to the updated terms.
